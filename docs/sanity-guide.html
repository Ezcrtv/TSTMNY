How to manage testimonies on your site

Every story on the site lives in Sanity Studio. You add and edit testimonies there, approve submissions, and the website updates itself. You never need to touch code.

Getting in

  1. Accept the email invite from Sanity and sign in with Google, GitHub, or email. Use the same login every time.

  2. Go to yourdomain.com/studio. Bookmark it. Your content lives here.

  3. On the left you'll see the content types: Testimony, Topic, Sport, and Site Settings. Click one to see its list, then click an item to open it.

The one rule: Approved + Published

A testimony only shows on the site when its Status is Approved and you have clicked Publish.

Changes appear on the live site within about a minute. If it's not there right away, wait a minute and refresh.

Pending means it's hidden. Use this for drafts and anything you're still reviewing. Approved means it's ready to be public.

Clicking Publish (bottom right) saves your edits to the live version. Until you publish, edits stay in a draft that only you can see in the Studio.

Add a new testimony

  1. Click Testimony, then the + / Create button at the top.

  2. Type the Title, then click Generate next to Slug. The slug becomes the page's web address, e.g. /testimony/the-day-i-came-back.

  3. Fill in the rest of the fields (see the table below). Only Title, Slug, Person name, and Status are required, but a story looks best with an image, excerpt, and pull quote.

  4. Set Status to Approved and click Publish.

FieldWhat to put there
TitleRequiredThe story's headline.
SlugRequiredClick Generate. Avoid changing it after publishing, since old links would break.
Person → NameRequiredWho the testimony belongs to. Add their photo if you have one.
SportPick from the list. If it's missing, create it under Sport first (see below).
LocationCity, Country.
ExcerptTwo or three sentences. Shown on story cards and at the top of the story.
Pull quoteOne short line from the story, shown large.
TopicsWhat the story is about: Faith, Injury, Family… Pick as many as fit.
Video URLPaste a YouTube or Vimeo link. Optional.
Lead imageThe main photo. Use a large, wide photo (2400px wide or more). Fill in Alt text with a short description of the photo for screen readers.
StoryThe full text. Use the H2 style for section headings and the Quote style for highlighted quotes.
StatusRequiredPending (hidden) or Approved (live).
FeaturedTurn on to pin this story to the homepage.
Published AtThe date shown on the story. Optional.

Tip on photos: after uploading an image, click the crop icon to set the hotspot, the part of the photo (usually a face) that should always stay visible when the site crops it.

Review stories people submit

When someone fills out "Share my testimony" on the Contact page, two things happen: you get an email, and a new Pending testimony appears in the Studio. Nothing pending is ever shown on the site.

  1. Open Testimony and find the new entry.

  2. Read it and tidy the text. Add a title, image, sport, topics, excerpt, and pull quote.

  3. When it's ready, set Status to Approved and Publish. If it's not a fit, leave it Pending or delete it.

Sports and topics

These power the filters on the Testimonies page. Adding one is a Studio entry, not a website change.

  1. Click Sport (or Topic) → Create.

  2. Type the name, e.g. "Basketball" or "Grief", click Generate for the slug, and Publish.

  3. Open a testimony and pick it in the Sport or Topics field.

A topic shows up as a filter once at least one approved story uses it. The sport filter appears once stories cover two or more sports.

Editing, hiding, and removing

  • Fix a typo: open the testimony, edit, click Publish.
  • Hide a story temporarily: set Status back to Pending and Publish. It disappears from the site but stays saved.
  • Remove for good: use the ⋯ menu next to Publish → Delete. This can't be undone, so hiding is usually safer.
  • Undo a mistake: the clock icon (top right of a document) shows its history and lets you restore an earlier version.

Things you can ignore

  • Fields marked (legacy), like Sport (legacy text), Themes (legacy), Category (legacy), and Short Description. They're from an older version. When you edit an older story, pick its Sport and Topics, then clear the legacy field.
  • Site Settings. The homepage text and social links are currently set in the website code. Ask your developer if you want to change them.
  • The Vision tab in the Studio. It's a developer tool.
